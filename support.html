Common questions

Does Site Slip work without internet?
Yes — everything, including generating PDF quotes, works fully offline.

Is my client data sent to a server?
No. All client and quote information stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How does the free trial work?
Site Slip includes a 7-day free trial when you first open the app. After that, a subscription ($2.99/month or $24.99/year) is required to continue generating new PDF quotes. Your quote history remains accessible either way.

I want to cancel my subscription.
Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID or Google Play account settings, not within Site Slip itself — this is standard for all app subscriptions. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions.
